Puberty and sex education

What happens during puberty

Puberty is when your body changes. Sex is something to talk about with a parent or a doctor. Masturbation is normal and healthy.

Staying healthy

Condoms and other contraception prevent pregnancy and infections such as HIV. Consent means saying yes freely. Your school nurse or clinic can help. Breast changes are normal; talk to a doctor about any lump, as breast cancer screening helps.
